# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated, with the publishing method confirmed by the owner: a static site (Astro) with Markdown content and a browser-based admin panel (Git-backed CMS at `/admin`) so new pieces, images, tables, and model files can be published without touching code. Hosting target undecided (free static host connected to GitHub, e.g. Netlify, Vercel, or Cloudflare Pages).

## Users

Broader investing readers: people interested in deals, valuation, companies, and markets who arrive through a shared link or search and read one piece closely. The career angle (investment banking recruiting) is secondary; a banker or recruiter who lands here should still see rigor and judgment, but the site is written for the curious investor, not as a résumé.

## Product Purpose

A personal publication for long-form investment writing and financial models (DCFs, three-statement models, comps, deal analyses). It exists to document a compounding effort to build knowledge and experience in investment banking-relevant areas, in public. Success: a reader finishes a piece, trusts the analysis, and comes back for the next one; the archive visibly grows over time.

## Positioning

A single author's working notebook of analysis, showing the numbers behind the opinions: each piece can carry its own charts, tables, and downloadable model, not just prose. The name and idea draw on Warren Buffett's *The Snowball*: knowledge compounds along a long curve.

## Operating Context

The owner writes pieces and builds models in Excel, exports charts and graphs as images, and publishes through the admin panel. Readers read on desktop and phone, often from a link shared on LinkedIn or by message.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two content kinds: Writing (essays, notes, deal or company analyses) and Models (a financial model with write-up, key outputs, and a downloadable file).
- Each piece supports images (charts, graphs, screenshots), tables, headings, quotes, and footnote-style notes.
- Publishing without code via the admin panel; content stored as Markdown in the repository.
- No comments, accounts, or paywall.
- Open: hosting provider, custom domain, newsletter/subscribe.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Moving Curve** (chosen by the owner).
- Published under the brand; the owner's name is not on the masthead, only on the About page.
- Inspiration: Warren Buffett and *The Snowball* (compounding, patience, long horizons).
- Must read as professional and credible to finance readers.
- Standing preference: the category standard, executed at full craft. The owner chose the clean independent-newsletter form (single author, essay-first, inline charts and tables) over a distinctive visual world. Sits alongside the Collaborative Fund blog (collabfund.com/blog) and finance newsletters like Net Interest and The Diff; their craft level is the bar. No smuggled quirk, but it must not look like an off-the-shelf blog theme.
- No email subscribe at launch.

## Evidence on Hand

No published pieces or models yet. Sample content shipped with the build is placeholder and must be labeled as such or replaced; do not invent credentials, employers, deal experience, returns, or readership figures.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the proof: show the numbers, sources, and model behind every claim.
2. Reading comes first; nothing competes with the text, chart, or table being read.
3. The archive should feel like it compounds: every new piece adds to a visible body of work.
4. Publishing must stay effortless, or the habit dies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Charts and tables need text alternatives (alt text, captions, real HTML tables rather than table screenshots where possible). Readable on phones.
