# Surface brief: Moving Curve publication (home feed, piece page, tag pages, about)

Scope: the whole reading site. Mode: Read.
Audience: broader investing readers arriving from a shared link; they read one piece closely, then browse the feed.
Job: understand an argument and the numbers behind it (charts, tables, downloadable model).
Constraints: one chronological feed with tags (no separate Models section); pieces with an attached model are marked as such; no subscribe at launch; owner name only on About.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The independent single-author finance newsletter, played straight at the craft level of the Collaborative Fund blog and Net Interest / The Diff. It refuses the off-the-shelf theme look (stock card grids, hero banners, Medium chrome) through typographic precision, not through a world costume.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, near-black ink, one deep blue accent for links and the primary chart series. Serif reading face for titles and body, a workhorse sans for meta, tables and UI, tabular numerals everywhere numbers appear. Hairline rules, no cards, no shadows. A small authored curve mark beside the wordmark. Light and dark themes from the same tokens.

STORY: The reader lands on a piece, reads comfortably at a real measure, meets charts and tables set as numbered exhibits in one consistent style, can download the model, and finds the next piece in the feed or by tag.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: slim header (mark + wordmark left, Archive by tag / About right), then the newest piece as a large serif headline with dek, date, read time, tags and model badge, then the dated feed as ruled rows. Piece page: header, title, dek, meta line, then the column at ~68ch.

FORM: Category standard (canon), chosen by the owner over the rolled direction; seed key 0eac412a.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
